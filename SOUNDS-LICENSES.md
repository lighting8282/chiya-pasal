# Sound licences

One row for every recorded sound in `sounds/`. Fill it in when you download a file: the file name, where it came from, who made it, the licence **as written on that sound's own page**, and the date. Keep a screenshot of the licence page too. Sounds must be CC0 / public domain or your own recordings (see `sounds/README.md`).

BigSoundBank (https://bigsoundbank.com) publishes its sounds as CC0 ("public domain equivalent"; free for commercial or non-commercial use, worldwide) and the recordist is Joseph Sardin. It doesn't allow scripted downloads, so download each file in a browser: open the sound's page, choose **OGG** in the drop-down under the player, then OK.

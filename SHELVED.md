# Shelved lessons

Lessons taken out of Habre's Nepali (`kids.html`) for now. They may come back, possibly as paid content. Nothing was deleted: the content is still in the file, and the `SHELVED` list near the top of the script switches each one off.

To bring one back, remove its id from `SHELVED` in `kids.html`. It then reappears everywhere it used to be.

Every room of the house keeps its picture and its look-and-find game; only the lessons under the rooms are shelved. The "More in this room" heading is hidden when a room has none. The look-and-find games still give stars (up to 8 a room), so the gate to the garden still opens at 10 stars.

## While shelved

- **What's left to play:** the rooms' look-and-find games, the journey (its trips, stories, checks and big checks), flashcards and memory match in the office, and daily practice, which now draws from the journey's word topics only.
- **Stories** are still read along the journey; only the bedroom's shelf of all stories is gone.
- **Story words** from shelved topics (for example *aama*) still show their meaning when tapped in a story.
- **Back buttons** that used to lead to the word list lead to the map instead while it is shelved.
- **Saved progress** for these lessons is kept, and counts again once they come back.
